# Global Superstore Sales and Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses Global Superstore sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional differences.

The aim is to identify business trends, highlight profitable areas, investigate losses, and provide insights that can support better business decisions.

## Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability.
- Examine sales and profit trends over time.
- Compare performance across product categories and sub-categories.
- Identify top-performing countries, products, and customers.
- Investigate loss-making products and regions.
- Provide recommendations based on the findings.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## Data Preparation and Modelling

The project involved data inspection, cleaning, transformation, and preparation for analysis.

A dimensional data model was developed using a central sales fact table and supporting dimension tables for products, customers, locations, and dates.

Measures were created to evaluate key performance indicators, including total sales, total profit, total orders, average profit, and average shipping cost.

## Key Findings

- Technology contributed approximately 40.1% of total sales, making it the largest of the three product categories by sales.
- The United States recorded the highest profit among the leading countries examined for 2014.
- Nigeria recorded a loss of $23,285.19 in the 2014 comparison, while Africa recorded a profit of $39,331.37.
- In Southeast Asia, Tables recorded a loss of $18,618.31.
- The analysis highlighted differences in profitability across products, countries, and customer segments.

## Recommendations

- Investigate products and regions with persistent losses.
- Review discounting practices where high discounts coincide with poor profitability.
- Prioritise products and markets that contribute positively to profit.
- Monitor sales and profitability together rather than relying on sales figures alone.
- Use customer and product performance insights to guide business decisions.

## Dashboard

The Power BI report presents the analysis through interactive visualisations, KPI cards, trend analysis, and product and regional comparisons.

The report file is available in this repository.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Power BI for data cleaning, data modelling, DAX calculations, visualisation, and business analysis. It shows how sales data can be transformed into insights that support informed decision-making.
